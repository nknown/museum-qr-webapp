MIYP is a museum platform for preparing museums, exhibitions, pieces, and visitor presentations.

Curators can organize content for on-site screens and handheld devices, while visitors can discover and revisit cultural material.

Imagined by:

MIYP stands for Museum in Your Pocket.

It combines Django and Wagtail to manage museum records, presentation pages, QR access, visitor navigation, and statistics.

The interface supports both desktop curation and smartphone presentation access.
